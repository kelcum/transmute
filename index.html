    What it converts

    Good to know

    Why is the first conversion of a type slower?
    Each converter downloads its engine the first time you use it (the video fallback engine is about 30 MB). After that your browser caches it.
    How accurate is Word → PDF?
    It rebuilds the document with clean typography: headings, lists, tables, images, and bold/italic text. It isn't a pixel-perfect copy of what Word shows.
    Are there size limits?
    Only your device's memory. Most videos convert without trouble, but multi-gigabyte files may be too big for phones or low-memory laptops.
    How accurate are extracted screenshots?
    For MP4, MOV, WebM, MKV and TS, they're the exact frame at each timestamp. Other formats fall back to a slightly slower engine with the same result.
